## Forest

This world has a unique place and purpose as the starting world (though on repeat playthroughs the player may unlock the ability to start in other worlds). The time between starting it and expanding to the second world will be much shorter than the time between the second world and the third or any gap after.
    
    - Visual: Green grass, rocky patches, teal ponds, some trees
    - Terrain: Mostly smooth, some patches of difficult, a few water ponds. Impassable terrain in thin curves (walls of trees)
    - Enemies:
        - Bandits (standard)
        - Goblin Bandits (swarming)
        - Bandit Cavalry (speedy)
        - Big Buff Bandit (tank, less affected by terrain)
        - Trained Falcons (Flying)
        - Dreaded Bandit Boss (Boss)
    - Resources: Wood (dropped), Ore (resource point)
    - Tower Modules:
        - Base: Wooden (Average aiming, 360° FoV, 80% range, 10 HP, c: 10 wood)
        - Base: Armored (Slow aiming, 90° FoV, 110% range, 50 HP, c: 2 wood 8 ore)
        - Core: Shaft (100% fire rate, 90% damage, 50% AoE, c: 10 wood)
        - Core: Chain Drive (150% fire rate, 80% damage, 25% AoE, c: 5 wood 5 ore)
        - Core: Weighted (80% fire rate, 120% damage, 120% AoE, c: 10 ore)
        - Head: Ballista (Moderate damage, AoE effects increase pierce, no splash, c: 10 wood)
        - Head: Trebuchet (Slow, decent damage, high range, decent splash, c: 5 wood 5 ore)
